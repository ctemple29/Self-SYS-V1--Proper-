• Nature and Architecture Explorer
  • Rules
    • Only the central part of the image is revealed first.
    • User must hover over other segments to reveal image.
    • Only examples of architecture and natural environments are displayed.
    • User must click to randomise selected display image.
  • Controls: Use the mouse to hover over empty portions, click to randomise selection.
  • Reflection
    • The system now has much more variety in the images displayed, has some basic styling and the fades are applied.
    • It doess a task and it does it in a relatively simple manner, it is not overly complicated and doesn't try to surpass its purpose.
    • The atmosphere does require work and presently feels mediocre and the trail could be gradually fine tuned.
    • One change I will make in V2 is spending much more time on the atmosphere. I understand that this is the weakest point of the system and could be refined, Using a more pleasant colour combination and text colouration could go a long way.
